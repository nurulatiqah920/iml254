READING

My second hobby is reading novels. I started to love reading novels when I was 13 years old. I read novel in my school library, SMKTR. I was reading romance novel titled, Kurang Asam Punya Bos! write by Nuroll Shazlinda Hairul. This novel genre is comedy romance. I enjoyed reading this novel, so I started to read and borrow as many as possible in my school library.


When I was 17 years old, I started to buy my own novel, titled Bidadari Tanpa Sayap by Ezza Mysara. Every time I have free time, I will fulfill my time by reading novels, including at school. Besides Malay novels, I also read English Novel with Fiction, Romance, and Islamic Literature genres, titled Will You Stay? and Will You Love Me? By Norhafsah Hamid. I feel peaceful while read these novels due to Islamic genre. These novels show how beautiful Islam is. As an Islam, I recommended book lovers to read her novel.


On my way to read her next novel!
